# Privacy Policy — KVM Client

*Last updated: October 22, 2026*

KVM Client does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs that track you.

- **Your devices.** The KVM names and addresses you save stay on your device, in the app's own storage. Passwords are kept in the system Keychain.
- **Network traffic.** The app connects only to the KVMs you add or that it finds on your local network. Video, keyboard and mouse data go directly between your device and your KVM; nothing passes through a server operated by us.
- **Local network search.** When you use *Search the network*, the app briefly contacts the addresses of your local network to recognize KVMs. The results are shown to you and are not sent anywhere.
- **Permissions.** Local Network access is used to reach your KVMs. On the GitHub build for macOS, the optional Accessibility permission is used only to forward system shortcuts such as ⌘Tab to the remote computer while its screen has focus.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/dumanhaydar/kvmclient/issues.
